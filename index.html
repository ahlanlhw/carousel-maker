Free carousel maker

  • Type or paste your text.
  • Get 4:5 slides for Instagram, TikTok and Threads.
  • One tab for each slide. Press Enter for a new line.
  • Pick the font, background, bold or italic for each slide.
  • Download them. Nothing leaves your browser.

Write your slides

  • Start with # to make a cover.
  • Leave a blank line between slides.
  • A short first line goes big. Long slides split on their own.
  • Up to 10 slides. This replaces the slides you have.

    How it posts

    • Instagram: up to 10 slides. 4:5 is the best size.
    • TikTok photo posts: every slide must be the same size. These all are.
    • Threads: up to 20 images in one post.
